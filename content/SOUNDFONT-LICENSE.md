## Track sources

### Ode to Joy

- Composition: Ludwig van Beethoven (public domain).
- MIDI and LilyPond source: [Mutopia Project, piece 528](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528), public domain.
- Reference recording: [Commons `Ode_to_Joy.ogg`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ode_to_Joy.ogg), public domain.
- Voice data, chart and rendered stems: CC0 1.0.

### Greensleeves

- Composition: traditional English folk song (public domain).
- MIDI: [Mutopia Project, piece 1265](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=1265), public domain.
- Chart and rendered stems: CC0 1.0.

### Minuet in G Major (Bach Minuet)

- Composition: "Minuet in G major", BWV Anh. 114, attributed to Christian Petzold (public domain).
- Source MIDI: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach_-_Minuet_in_G_Major.mid), public domain (Mutopia Project upload).
- Highest-voice melody reduction, chart and rendered stems: CC0 1.0.

### Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star

- Melody: traditional (public domain).
- Reference recording: [Commons OGG](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Twinkle_Twinkle_Little_Star_plain.ogg), public domain.
- Arrangement, chart and rendered stems: original work for this project, released CC0 1.0.

The rendered arrangements and performances are released as CC0 1.0. The
soundfont samples and notices above remain subject to the MIT licence and must
be retained.
