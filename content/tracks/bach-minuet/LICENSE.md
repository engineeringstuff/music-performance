# Minuet in G Major — provenance

- **Composition:** "Minuet in G major", BWV Anh. 114, formerly attributed to J. S. Bach and
  now generally attributed to Christian Petzold (public domain).
- **Source MIDI:** [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bach_-_Minuet_in_G_Major.mid),
  public domain. The source is committed unchanged as `source.mid`; its SHA-256 is recorded
  in `track.json`.
- **Reduction:** the right-hand track was reduced to a single highest-voice melody for the
  chart; the left-hand track is the backing. This is an editorial reduction for a
  monophonic game, committed as `voices.json`.
- **Outputs:** `melody.mid`, `backing.mid`, the chart and the arrangement are **CC0 1.0**.
